## A. Build output — delete or exclude (25.5 MB)

These are compiler/linker artefacts. They are regenerated by every build, they are
not source, and shipping them makes the submission look like a build dump.

| Path | Size | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| `x64/` | ~12.5 MB | **Delete the whole folder.** Contains `Debug/`, `.obj`, `.pdb`, `.ilk`, `.tlog/`, `.idb`, `.exe`, `.recipe`, `.lastbuildstate`, `.log` |
| `neoverse cpp/` (the nested folder) | ~13.0 MB | **Delete the whole folder.** Contains only a second copy of `x64\Debug\` — it holds no source files at all |
| `.vs/` | — | Delete if present (created when you open the solution in VS) |

Sanity check: `neoverse cpp/neoverse cpp.vcxproj` sits in the **root**, not in the
nested `neoverse cpp/` folder. Deleting the nested folder removes nothing you need.
